Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Metrics Sync Utility is a private reporting utility.

Google user data accessed

After explicit Google OAuth authorization, the application may access:

  • Read-only YouTube account information
  • Non-monetary YouTube Analytics reports

Use of data

The data is used solely to generate private aggregate reporting for the authorized user.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties and is not made publicly available by this website.

Permissions not requested

The application does not upload, edit, or delete YouTube content, manage comments, or access YouTube revenue data.

Credentials

OAuth credentials and private reporting data are not published on this website.

Revoking access

Users may revoke the application's access at any time through their Google Account settings.
